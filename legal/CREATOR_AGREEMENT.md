## 1. Engagement

The Agency engages the Creator as an **independent contractor** to produce and publish influencer marketing content for clients of the Agency (each, a "Campaign"). The Creator accepts this engagement on the terms below.

The Creator is **not an employee, partner, joint venturer, or agent** of the Agency. The Creator has no authority to bind the Agency.

## 2. Campaign Process

### 2.1 Booking
Campaigns are offered to the Creator through the Agency platform, email, or direct message. The Creator may accept or decline any individual Campaign.

### 2.2 Brief
For each accepted Campaign, the Agency will provide a brief including:
- Client name
- Deliverables (number, platform, length, format)
- Creative direction and required talking points
- Delivery deadline
- Posting schedule
- Usage rights granted to the client

### 2.3 Content Production
The Creator will:
- Produce content matching the brief in quality consistent with the Creator's public portfolio
- Follow the brief's creative direction and required talking points
- Complete one round of revisions within 48 hours if requested
- Deliver content by the agreed deadline

### 2.4 Posting
The Creator will post approved content on the Creator's own social accounts at the time specified in the brief, using the required captions, hashtags, and tags. Posts must remain live for the minimum period specified in the brief (default: **90 days**).

## 3. Compensation

### 3.1 Revenue Share
For each Campaign the Creator completes, the Creator will receive **50% of the gross revenue** received by the Agency for that Campaign, calculated after payment-processor fees (e.g., Stripe ~2.9% + $0.30).

### 3.2 Payment Timing
Payment will be issued to the Creator within **7 business days** after:
1. The client's campaign is marked delivered by the Agency, AND
2. The client's payment has fully cleared (no pending Stripe payouts, chargebacks, or disputes)

### 3.3 Payment Method
Payment will be made by ACH, Stripe Connect, Wise, PayPal, or other method mutually agreed. The Creator is responsible for providing accurate payment details.

### 3.4 Taxes
The Creator is responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, and business taxes on amounts received. The Agency will issue **IRS Form 1099-NEC** for any tax year in which the Creator is paid $600 or more.

### 3.5 Chargebacks / Refunds
If a client disputes or charges back a Campaign payment after the Creator has been paid, the Creator will reimburse their 50% share within **30 days** of notice, OR the amount may be offset against future earnings.

## 4. Content Usage Rights

### 4.1 Grant to Client
For each Campaign, the Creator grants the Agency (and through the Agency, the client) a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license** to use the Campaign content for marketing purposes for the duration specified in the brief. Default durations:

| Package Tier | Client Usage Rights Term |
|---|---|
| Single Reel / Dual Post | 90 days |
| Content Pack / Brand Deal | 6 months |
| 3-Creator Collab / 5-Creator Rollout | 6 months |
| 10-Creator Launch / Feed Takeover | 12 months |
| Agency Campaign / Influencer Network | Perpetual / lifetime (as specified in brief) |

### 4.2 Creator Ownership
The Creator retains ownership of all Campaign content created. The license in §4.1 is a usage license only, not a transfer of copyright.

### 4.3 Portfolio Use
The Creator may display Campaign content in their personal portfolio, case studies, and reel showcases, unless a specific Campaign brief prohibits this.

### 4.4 Content Removal
The Creator will not delete or make private any Campaign post before the minimum display period (default 90 days) without Agency written approval. Early removal may result in a clawback of the Creator's fee for that Campaign.

## 5. Exclusivity

### 5.1 Category Exclusivity (Per Campaign)
During a Campaign's live period, the Creator will not post paid content for a **direct competitor** of the client in the same product category, unless otherwise approved in writing. The brief will specify which competitors, if any, are restricted.

### 5.2 General Non-Exclusivity
The Creator is free to work with other agencies, brands, and clients directly, and to accept any booking that does not conflict with §5.1.

## 6. Representations & Standards

The Creator represents and warrants:

### 6.1 Authenticity
- Followers are real and organic — the Creator has not purchased fake followers, bots, or paid-engagement services
- Follower count and engagement metrics provided to the Agency are accurate within reasonable rounding

### 6.2 Compliance
- All posts will include required **FTC disclosure tags** (`#ad`, `#sponsored`, `Paid Partnership with`, etc.) as required by law
- Content will comply with each platform's community guidelines (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)
- Content will not infringe any third-party copyright, trademark, right of publicity, or other intellectual property right

### 6.3 Conduct
- The Creator will not include content that is defamatory, hateful, sexually explicit (unless the brief specifically calls for adult content and the Creator is on appropriate platforms), violent, or illegal
- The Creator will represent the client and Agency professionally and will not make public statements disparaging either

### 6.4 Authority
- The Creator is at least 18 years old
- The Creator has full legal right to enter this Agreement and to grant the usage rights in §4

## 7. Termination

### 7.1 Termination by Either Party
Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time with **14 days written notice**, provided that all accepted Campaigns already in progress are completed.

### 7.2 Immediate Termination
The Agency may terminate immediately (and the Creator forfeits unpaid fees for any incomplete Campaign) if the Creator:
- Materially breaches §6 (Representations & Standards)
- Fails to deliver content for two consecutive Campaigns without reasonable cause
- Engages in conduct that materially damages the Agency's reputation
- Is found to have purchased fake followers, bots, or engagement

### 7.3 Post-Termination
After termination:
- The Creator remains bound by §4 (Usage Rights), §6 (Representations), §8 (Confidentiality) for all completed Campaigns
- Any Campaigns already paid by clients continue to be honored — Creator still delivers; Agency still pays

## 8. Confidentiality

The Creator will keep confidential all non-public information received from the Agency or its clients, including client names, brief contents, pricing, and campaign performance, both during and for **2 years after** this Agreement ends.

## 9. Independent Contractor Status

The Creator:
- Sets their own hours and working conditions
- Uses their own equipment
- Is not eligible for employee benefits (health insurance, PTO, workers' comp, unemployment, etc.)
- Is solely responsible for self-employment taxes
- May work for other agencies and clients simultaneously (subject to §5)

## 10. Indemnification

The Creator will indemnify and hold harmless the Agency and its clients against any claim, loss, or liability arising from:
- The Creator's breach of this Agreement
- The Creator's content that infringes a third-party right
- The Creator's misrepresentation of follower authenticity or metrics
- The Creator's failure to comply with FTC disclosure requirements

## 11. Limitation of Liability

The Agency's liability to the Creator under this Agreement is limited to the amount of unpaid Campaign fees owed to the Creator at the time of any claim. Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or lost-profits damages.

## 12. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute will first be addressed by good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, disputes will be submitted to **binding arbitration** in [CITY, STATE] under the American Arbitration Association rules. Each Party bears its own fees unless the arbitrator awards otherwise.

## 13. Governing Law

## 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with each individual Campaign brief, is the entire agreement between the Parties. Amendments require written signatures of both Parties.

## 15. Assignment

The Creator may not assign this Agreement or delegate obligations to another person without written consent from the Agency. The Agency may assign this Agreement to a successor business entity.
