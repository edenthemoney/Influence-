## 1. Purpose

The Parties enter this Agreement to jointly operate **Influence Models Agency**, an influencer marketing and talent booking platform (the "Business"). This Agreement governs revenue splits, roles, responsibilities, decision-making, and dissolution between the Parties.

## 2. Revenue Allocation

For every paid campaign booked through the platform (whether via Stripe, bank transfer, invoice, or any other payment method), gross revenue is allocated as follows:

| Recipient | Percentage | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Model / Creator** (the influencer performing the work) | **50%** | Compensation for content creation, posting, and usage rights |
| **Partner — Deseray** | **15%** | Ongoing partnership, roster-building, model relationships, and brand representation |
| **Agency — Eden** | **35%** | Operating costs including employee salaries, software/hosting, advertising, legal, accounting, business taxes, support, refunds, chargebacks, and reinvestment |

### 2.1 Definition of "Gross Revenue"
Gross revenue means the **total amount received after payment-processor fees** (e.g., Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30). Refunds, chargebacks, and disputed payments reduce gross revenue before splits are calculated.

### 2.2 Expense Responsibility
The 35% Agency share is intended to cover **all shared operating expenses**. Deseray's 15% share is not subject to further deduction for operating expenses unless both Parties agree in writing.

### 2.3 Payment Schedule
- Model payouts: within **7 business days** of the client's campaign being marked delivered and the Stripe payment clearing (typically 2-7 days after charge).
- Deseray's partner share: paid **bi-weekly** (every other Friday) by ACH or method mutually agreed upon, covering all completed campaigns in the prior period.
- Agency retains its 35% in the business operating account.

### 2.4 Reporting
Eden will provide Deseray a **monthly statement** (by the 5th of each month) showing:
- Gross revenue received
- Per-campaign breakdown (campaign name, model, gross amount, each party's share)
- Year-to-date totals

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### 3.1 Eden (Agency Owner)
- Owns and operates the `influencemodels.agency` domain, website, Stripe account, and business bank account
- Manages all technical infrastructure, hosting, website code, and third-party integrations
- Handles customer support, dispute resolution, and refunds
- Responsible for business tax filings, corporate compliance, and bookkeeping
- Manages advertising, marketing spend, and SEO
- Onboards and manages any employees or contractors working for the Agency

### 3.2 Deseray (Partner)
- Recruits and onboards new models to the platform roster
- Maintains relationships with existing models and serves as primary model liaison
- Represents the brand publicly (content, press, partnerships)
- Provides creative input on campaigns, messaging, and positioning
- May pitch her own audience as a lead source for the Business

### 3.3 Models / Creators
- Are **independent contractors** of the Business — not employees and not partners
- Sign separate **Creator Agreements** governing their individual bookings
- Retain ownership of their content except for agreed usage rights

## 4. Ownership & Intellectual Property

### 4.1 Business Assets
All business assets — including the `influencemodels.agency` domain, website code, Stripe account, customer data, client list, trademarks, and brand identity — are owned by the **Business entity** (see Section 11 below). No single Party owns these assets outright.

### 4.2 Pre-Existing Assets
Each Party retains ownership of their personal social media accounts, personal brand, and any work product created before the Effective Date.

### 4.3 Model Content
Content produced by models for client campaigns is governed by the separate Creator Agreement between the Business and each model — not by this Agreement.

## 5. Decision-Making

- **Day-to-day operations:** Eden has sole discretion (website changes, marketing spend, support decisions, pricing adjustments within existing package structure).
- **Major decisions** require **written consent of both Parties**, including:
  - Adding or removing revenue-share recipients
  - Changing the revenue split percentages
  - Taking on debt or outside investment
  - Selling or transferring the Business or its assets
  - Hiring a full-time employee with equity/profit share
  - Any decision materially affecting the other Party's income

## 6. Time Commitment

Neither Party is required to work full-time on the Business. Each Party commits to acting in good faith and giving reasonable effort to their assigned responsibilities.

## 7. Exclusivity & Non-Compete

### 7.1 During the Agreement
Neither Party will operate a **directly competing influencer-marketing agency** during the term of this Agreement. Both Parties may continue their existing personal brand, modeling, or creator work.

### 7.2 After Termination
For **12 months** after this Agreement ends, neither Party will solicit existing clients or models of the Business for a competing venture.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep confidential all non-public Business information, including client lists, pricing data, financial records, and model contracts — both during and for **3 years after** the term of this Agreement.

## 9. Tax Responsibility

- Each Party is responsible for their own federal, state, and local income taxes on amounts received under this Agreement.
- Eden will issue **Form 1099-NEC** (or equivalent) to Deseray each year reflecting total amounts paid.
- The Agency's 35% share is subject to business-level taxes handled by Eden under the Business's tax structure (see Section 11).

## 10. Term & Termination

### 10.1 Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated per this Section.

### 10.2 Termination for Convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement with **60 days' written notice** to the other Party.

### 10.3 Termination for Cause
Either Party may terminate **immediately** upon written notice if the other Party:
- Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 15 days
- Commits fraud, theft, or other act materially damaging the Business
- Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

### 10.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- All campaigns already booked and paid are honored; Deseray receives her 15% on those bookings even if delivered after termination
- No future bookings generate Deseray's share after the termination effective date
- Confidentiality and non-solicit clauses (Sections 7.2, 8) survive
- Eden retains ownership of all Business assets (domain, code, Stripe account)

## 11. Business Entity (To Be Decided)

**☐ Option A — Sole Proprietorship / DBA (current default)**
Eden operates as a sole proprietor. Deseray is paid as a **1099 independent contractor** at 15% of gross. Simplest setup, no partnership tax return required.

**☐ Option B — LLC with Eden as sole member**
Eden forms an LLC; Deseray is paid as a 1099 contractor. Provides liability protection for Eden. Low complexity.

**☐ Option C — Multi-member LLC (true partnership)**
Both Parties are LLC members. Requires partnership tax return (Form 1065) and K-1s. More complex but gives Deseray direct ownership stake.

> **Recommendation:** Start with **Option A or B** and convert to Option C later if the Business scales. Discuss with a CPA.
