## Outreach Strategy

### Step 1: Brand Research
- Find brands with 10K-500K followers (not too big, not too small)
- Check if they're currently doing influencer marketing
- Look for brands with recent product launches
- Identify brands with visual products/services

### Step 2: Decision Maker Identification
- **CEO/Founder** - For smaller brands (<50 employees)
- **CMO/Marketing Director** - For mid-sized brands (50-500 employees)
- **Brand Manager** - For larger brands (500+ employees)
- **E-commerce Manager** - For retail brands

### Step 3: Personalized Pitch Creation
- Reference their recent content/campaigns
- Suggest specific influencers from your roster
- Show relevant case studies
- Include clear call-to-action

### Step 4: Multi-Touch Outreach
- **Email 1**: Introduction + value proposition
- **Email 2**: Case study + specific influencer suggestions
- **Email 3**: Social proof + urgency
- **LinkedIn**: Connect + message
- **Follow-up**: 3-5 day intervals

## Compliance & Best Practices

- **GDPR**: Include unsubscribe link
- **CAN-SPAM**: Include physical address
- **Personalization**: Minimum 3 personalized elements per email
- **Frequency**: Max 1 email every 3-5 days
- **List Quality**: Verify emails before sending
- **Spam Score**: Keep below 5/10
