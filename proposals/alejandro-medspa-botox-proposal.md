## 1. Research Snapshot: Why This Works for a Med Spa

- **Micro-influencers (10K–50K)** drive the highest ROI for local med spas: higher engagement, lower cost per lead, and followers who can actually book a consultation.
- **45–90 second Reels** that show the full treatment journey (consultation → procedure → real reaction → soft CTA) consistently outperform static ads and become reusable ad creative for 6–12 months.
- **Whitelisting** = running paid ads from the creator’s handle. These consistently outperform branded ads **2–4x** on cost-per-consultation.
- Med-spa influencer content builds trust because potential patients see a real person experiencing the treatment — critical for Botox where skepticism is high.
- Industry benchmark for micro-influencer aesthetic partnerships: **$200–$1,500 per post/reel + treatment**, with mid-tier (50K–250K) ranging **$1,000–$5,000+ per post**. Premium agencies typically build in 2.5x–4x markup for casting, compliance, editing, usage rights, and campaign management.

## 6. What Makes the Content Convert

Based on med-spa best practices, each Reel should include:
1. **Hook in first 3 seconds** — “I finally tried Botox and this is what actually happened.”
2. **Consultation clip** — provider explains the treatment.
3. **Treatment B-roll** — close-ups without graphic detail; focus on professionalism.
4. **Real-time reaction** — immediate, authentic post-treatment response.
5. **Subtle results / glow** — natural, not overdone.
6. **Soft CTA** — “DM Alejandro’s med spa to book your consultation.”
7. **FTC disclosure** — #ad / paid partnership visible.
