## 1. Purpose

The Parties enter this Agreement to jointly operate Influence Models Agency, an influencer marketing, talent booking, and content production platform (the "Business"). This Agreement governs revenue splits, roles, responsibilities, decision-making, and dissolution between the Parties.

## 2. Services Offered

The Business offers five (5) distinct service categories:

| Service | Description | Delivery |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Music Reactions** | Models listen and react to client music on camera | Remote — HD video delivered |
| **UGC & Reels** | Models create branded short-form content (skits, promos, product videos) to client brief | Remote — HD video delivered |
| **Brand Ambassadors** | Models visit client's business as brand ambassadors to create on-site promotional reels and social content | In-Person — South Florida |
| **Shoots & Videos** | Models appear on-location for music videos, brand campaigns, and photo shoots | In-Person — South Florida |
| **Events & Hosting** | Models host, promote, or attend client events as brand ambassadors | In-Person — South Florida |

Each service is sold as either a **one-time booking** or a **monthly subscription**. Pricing is set by the Agency via published package tiers (see Appendix B).

## 3. Revenue Allocation

### 3.1 Compensation Model Overview

The Business uses **two compensation structures** depending on booking type:

| Booking Type | Model Compensation | Remaining Revenue Split |
|-------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **One-Time Packages** | 50% of gross revenue | Deseray 15% / Agency 35% |
| **Monthly Subscriptions, Bulk, & Custom Packages** | Pre-approved flat rate per deliverable | Deseray 15% / Agency 85% of remaining revenue after model payouts |

Both structures are detailed below.

---

### 3.2 One-Time Package Split (Standard)

For every **one-time** paid booking, gross revenue is allocated as follows:

| Recipient | Percentage | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Model / Creator | 50% | Compensation for content creation, appearance, performance, and usage rights |
| Partner — Deseray | 15% | Partnership, roster-building, model relationships, and brand representation |
| Agency — Eden | 35% | Operating costs including salaries, software, hosting, advertising, legal, accounting, taxes, support, refunds, chargebacks, and reinvestment |

**Examples:**

- A **$500 UGC 3-Reel Pack** → Model $250 / Deseray $75 / Agency $175
- A **$650 Trio Shoot** → Model $325 / Deseray $97.50 / Agency $227.50
- A **$250 Single Reaction** → Model $125 / Deseray $37.50 / Agency $87.50

When a one-time package involves **multiple models**, the 50% model share is divided equally among participating models unless otherwise specified in the booking confirmation.

---

### 3.3 Monthly Subscriptions & Bulk Package Split (Per-Deliverable Model Rate)

For **monthly subscriptions**, **bulk custom packages**, and any engagement where models produce content at volume, models are compensated at a **pre-approved flat rate per deliverable** rather than a percentage of the total package price. This ensures fair, predictable model pay regardless of how the package is priced to the client.

**How it works:**

1. Before the campaign begins, both Parties agree on the **per-deliverable model rate** for that engagement (e.g., $50/reel, $75/reaction, $150/shoot day, $125/event appearance)
2. Models are paid their flat rate for each unit of work completed
3. The **remaining revenue** (gross revenue minus total model payouts) is split between the Parties:
   - **Deseray: 15%** of remaining revenue
   - **Agency: 85%** of remaining revenue

**Examples:**

- **Monthly UGC subscription** — Client pays $1,200/mo for 8 reels from 2 models. Pre-approved model rate: $50/reel.
  - Total model payout: 8 reels × $50 = $400
  - Remaining revenue: $1,200 − $400 = $800
  - Deseray: $800 × 15% = **$120**
  - Agency: $800 × 85% = **$680**

- **Bulk reaction campaign** — Client pays $4,000 for 25 influencers × 1 reaction each. Pre-approved model rate: $75/reaction.
  - Total model payout: 25 × $75 = $1,875
  - Remaining revenue: $4,000 − $1,875 = $2,125
  - Deseray: $2,125 × 15% = **$318.75**
  - Agency: $2,125 × 85% = **$1,806.25**

- **Monthly event hosting** — Client pays $3,000/mo for 4 events × 3 models each. Pre-approved model rate: $125/appearance.
  - Total model payout: 12 appearances × $125 = $1,500
  - Remaining revenue: $3,000 − $1,500 = $1,500
  - Deseray: $1,500 × 15% = **$225**
  - Agency: $1,500 × 85% = **$1,275**

### 3.4 Pre-Approved Rate Requirements

- Per-deliverable model rates must be **agreed upon in writing** by both Parties before the campaign or subscription begins
- Rates are documented in a simple Rate Sheet (email confirmation is sufficient)
- Either Party may propose rate adjustments; changes take effect on the next billing cycle
- Model rates may vary by service type, deliverable complexity, and model tier
- Both Parties must approve any rate below the **minimum floors** defined in Appendix B.9

### 3.5 In-Person Bookings (Shoots & Events) — Flexible Structure

For in-person bookings, either compensation method may apply:

- **One-time shoot/event:** Standard 50/15/35 percentage split (Section 3.2)
- **Recurring or bulk in-person bookings:** Pre-approved **per-day or per-appearance model rate**, with remaining revenue split 30/70 (Section 3.3)

The method is determined by booking type. If a client books a single shoot, the standard split applies. If a client has a monthly retainer for recurring shoots or events, both Parties agree on a per-day/per-appearance model rate in advance.

### 3.6 Definition of "Gross Revenue"

Gross revenue means the total amount received **after** payment-processor fees (e.g., Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30). Refunds, chargebacks, and disputed payments reduce gross revenue before splits are calculated.

### 3.7 Custom / Enterprise Bookings

For any custom engagement that does not fit a published package:

- Both Parties agree on the model compensation structure (percentage or flat rate) **before** the engagement begins
- If flat rate: remaining revenue splits 15% Deseray / 85% Agency
- If no agreement is reached, the standard 50/15/35 split applies as default

### 3.8 Operating Reserve

Before any revenue splits are calculated, the Agency retains a **5% operating reserve** from gross revenue to cover:

- Client refunds and chargebacks
- Payment processing disputes
- Legal fees and compliance costs
- Model no-shows or failed deliverables requiring client credits
- Insurance and liability costs

The reserve is deducted **before** the percentage or flat-rate splits are applied. If the reserve account exceeds $10,000 at the end of any calendar quarter, amounts above $10,000 are redistributed using the applicable split for that period. Eden manages the reserve at his sole discretion. Reserve expenditures are summarized as a total in the monthly statement alongside the reserve balance.

**Adjusted calculation example (one-time):**
- Client pays $500 → 5% reserve ($25) → Gross for split: $475
- Model 50%: $237.50 / Deseray 15%: $71.25 / Agency 35%: $166.25

### 3.9 Expense Responsibility

The Agency share (35% on one-time; 85% on subscription remaining) covers all shared operating expenses. Deseray's share is not subject to further deduction for operating expenses unless both Parties agree in writing. For the avoidance of doubt, the 5% operating reserve (Section 3.8) is a separate deduction and is not considered part of any Party's share.

### 3.10 Payment Schedule

- **Model payouts:** Within 7 business days of the campaign being marked delivered and the payment clearing (typically 2–7 days after charge)
- **Deseray's partner share:** Paid bi-weekly (every other Friday) by ACH or method mutually agreed upon, covering all completed bookings in the prior period
- **Agency:** Retains its share in the business operating account
- **Hold period:** Payments to all parties are subject to a 14-day hold from date of client payment to account for potential chargebacks or refund requests. If a chargeback or refund occurs during the hold period, the affected booking's splits are reversed accordingly.

### 3.11 Reporting

Eden will provide Deseray a monthly statement (by the 5th of each month) showing:

- Gross revenue received
- Operating reserve deductions
- Per-booking breakdown: service type, package name, model(s), gross amount, each party's share
- Breakdown by service category (Music Reactions, UGC & Reels, Shoots & Videos, Events & Hosting)
- Subscription revenue separated from one-time bookings
- Reserve fund balance
- Year-to-date totals

### 3.12 Agency Compensation

Eden has sole discretion over how the Agency's share is allocated between operating expenses and personal compensation. The monthly statement will report total Agency share received and total operating expenses as a single line item. Eden is not required to itemize individual operating expenses or disclose personal draw amounts.

## 4. Pricing Authority

### 4.1 Package Pricing

Eden has **sole discretion** to set, adjust, and publish package pricing for all service categories at any time. Deseray will be notified of material pricing changes within 7 days of implementation. Pricing changes do not require Partner approval.

### 4.2 Discounts & Promotions

Eden may offer promotional discounts of up to **30%** off published package prices at his sole discretion. Discounts exceeding 30% require written agreement from both Parties. The revenue split applies to the **actual amount collected** (i.e., after discount). Free or complimentary bookings (100% discount) do not generate any Party's share.

### 4.3 New Services & Service Modifications

Eden may add, modify, rebrand, or discontinue service categories at his sole discretion as part of normal business operations. Deseray will be notified of new service categories within 7 days of launch. The revenue split structure defined in Section 3 automatically applies to any new service unless both Parties agree in writing to a different structure.

### 4.4 Bundle Pricing & Loss Leaders

Eden may create promotional bundles, introductory offers, or loss-leader pricing to acquire new clients. If a bundle is priced below the combined model payout cost, the shortfall is absorbed by the Agency share — Deseray's share is calculated on the remaining revenue after model payouts (which may be $0 on loss-leader promotions). Loss-leader pricing may not be used on more than 15% of total bookings in any calendar month. Eden will notify Deseray when loss-leader pricing is actively in use.

## 13. Term & Termination

### 13.1 Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated per this Section.

### 13.2 Termination for Convenience

Either Party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days' written notice to the other Party.

### 13.3 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party:

- Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 15 days
- Commits fraud, theft, or other act materially damaging the Business
- Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

### 13.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:

- All bookings already paid are honored; Deseray receives her 15% on those bookings even if delivered after termination
- Active monthly subscriptions: Deseray receives her share through the end of the current billing cycle only
- No future bookings generate Deseray's share after the termination effective date
- Confidentiality and non-solicit clauses (Sections 9.2, 10) survive
- Eden retains ownership of all Business assets (domain, code, Stripe account)

### 13.5 Mutual Protection Against Misconduct

**Protecting Deseray:** If Eden misrepresents revenue or withholds payments owed under this Agreement, Deseray may pursue the full amount owed plus any resulting damages through arbitration under Section 14. Termination does not cut off Deseray's right to recover amounts owed from bookings completed before the termination date.

**Protecting Eden & the Agency:** If Deseray solicits or redirects Business clients or models to a competing service, discloses confidential information, or publicly disparages the Business, Eden will provide written notice of the alleged breach. If the issue is not resolved within 15 days, Eden may suspend future revenue share payments pending arbitration and pursue damages. Any revenue share accrued but not yet paid may be held in escrow pending resolution.

### 13.6 Financial Review Rights

Deseray may request an independent review of the Business's financial records **once per calendar year**. The cost of the review is split equally between the Parties. If the review reveals a discrepancy of more than 5% in Deseray's favor, Eden will cover the full cost of the review and immediately pay the shortfall. Eden will cooperate with any properly requested review within 30 days. Requests for additional reviews beyond one per year require mutual agreement.

### 13.7 Indemnification

Each Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other Party from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from their own negligence, misconduct, or breach of this Agreement. Neither Party is liable for the other Party's personal tax obligations, legal disputes unrelated to the Business, or actions taken outside the scope of this Agreement.
