## Job Description:

```
We're a luxury influencer marketing agency looking for MULTIPLE freelancers with existing Instagram/TikTok repost networks across different niches.

🎯 NICHES WE NEED (Apply for ONE niche only):
• Music / Hip-Hop / R&B
• Fitness / Gym / Bodybuilding
• Fashion / Beauty / Lifestyle
• Gaming / Tech / Esports
• Food / Cooking / Restaurants
• Travel / Adventure
• Business / Entrepreneurship / Motivation
• Sports / Athletics

📋 WHAT WE NEED PER NICHE:
• Existing network of 30-100+ Instagram/TikTok accounts
• Each account must have 5,000+ real followers
• Real engagement (NOT bots or fake accounts)
• Ability to repost content within 24 hours
• Accounts must be in ONE specific niche

💼 HOW IT WORKS:
1. We send you viral content when we have a client in your niche
2. You distribute and repost across your entire network
3. All accounts post within 24-48 hours (coordinated launch)
4. We pay you immediately after delivery via Upwork escrow
5. This is ongoing work - we have 2-5 campaigns per month per niche

💰 RATES (Per Campaign):
• 30-50 accounts: $150-200
• 50-100 accounts: $250-350
• 100+ accounts: $400-500
• Negotiable for larger networks

📝 TO APPLY, YOU MUST INCLUDE:

1. YOUR SPECIFIC NICHE (pick ONE from the list above)

2. NETWORK DETAILS:
   - Total number of accounts you manage
   - Platform (Instagram, TikTok, or both)
   - Average follower count per account
   - Total combined reach

3. PROOF OF NETWORK:
   - Screenshots of your accounts (can blur account names for privacy)
   - Previous campaign results/analytics
   - Engagement rate examples
   - Portfolio of past repost campaigns

4. RATES & AVAILABILITY:
   - Your per-campaign rate based on your network size
   - Turnaround time (24 or 48 hours)
   - How many campaigns per month can you handle
   - Your availability to start

⚠️ IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS:
• This is PER-CAMPAIGN work (not a monthly retainer)
• You only get paid when we send you campaigns
• Must be able to start within 24 hours of our request
• Payment via Upwork escrow for both parties' protection
• Must provide proof of real accounts (we will verify)
• No bots, no fake engagement, no spam accounts

🚫 DO NOT APPLY IF:
• You don't have an existing network (we're not hiring to build one)
• Your accounts are bots or have fake followers
• You can't provide proof of previous campaigns
• You can't deliver within 24-48 hours

✅ IDEAL CANDIDATES:
• Social media managers who already manage multiple accounts
• Influencer network owners
• Fan page operators
• Social media agencies with account portfolios
• Anyone with proven repost network experience

🎯 WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
1. We review applications and shortlist candidates
2. We'll ask for more detailed proof of your network
3. We may request a small test campaign ($50-100) before full campaigns
4. Once verified, you'll be added to our roster of trusted partners
5. We'll contact you when we have campaigns in your niche

💎 WHY WORK WITH US:
• Fast payment (within 24 hours of delivery)
• Ongoing work (we're a growing agency)
• Professional, easy to work with
• Clear communication and expectations
• Opportunity to scale as we grow

📧 QUESTIONS WE'LL ASK IN INTERVIEW:
• Can you show us a list of your accounts?
• What's your average engagement rate?
• How do you avoid getting accounts banned?
• Can you handle coordinated launches (all accounts post at same time)?
• Do you use automation tools or manual posting?

We're hiring MULTIPLE people (one specialist per niche), so if you have a strong network in ANY of the niches listed, please apply!

Looking forward to building long-term partnerships with reliable repost network managers.
```
