## ⚠️ Risks & How to Mitigate

### **Risk 1: Accounts Get Banned**
**Solution:**
- Use proxies (different IP per account)
- Don't post same content at exact same time
- Stagger posts (15-30 min apart)
- Use Jarvee's "human simulation" mode

### **Risk 2: Accounts Lose Followers**
**Solution:**
- Keep accounts active (post daily)
- Engage with other accounts
- Don't just repost - mix in original content

### **Risk 3: Upfront Cost Too High**
**Solution:**
- Start with 10 accounts ($1K-2K)
- Prove ROI
- Scale to 50-100 accounts
